# Filament Studio — User Guide

Welcome. This guide is for the people who *use* Filament Studio day to day: content editors, operations staff, and the administrators who set things up. It assumes no programming knowledge. There is no code in it.

If you are a developer installing the package, you want the [technical documentation](../../README.md) instead.

## What Filament Studio does, in one paragraph

Most business software has its structure decided by a programmer. If you want to record one more piece of information about a customer, you file a ticket and wait. Filament Studio removes that wait. You describe what you want to store — "a customer has a name, an email, and a status" — and Studio immediately builds the forms, the lists, the search filters, the reports and the permissions to match. You do this by filling in a few forms, not by writing code.

## The four ideas you need

Almost everything in Studio is built from four ideas. Learn these and the rest follows.

| Idea | Think of it as | Example |
|------|----------------|---------|
| **Collection** | A spreadsheet, or a filing cabinet drawer | "Customers" |
| **Field** | A column in that spreadsheet | "Email address" |
| **Record** | A single row | One particular customer |
| **Dashboard** | A summary page of charts and numbers | "Sales Overview" |

A collection holds records. Each record has a value for each field. That's it.

## A note on what you'll see

The screenshots in this guide come from a demo panel containing three collections — **Customers**, **Products** and **Orders**. Your panel will show your own collections, and your organisation may have renamed things or hidden sections you don't have permission to use. The shapes will match even when the words don't.

If a screen in this guide doesn't appear in your panel at all, it's usually because your account doesn't have permission for it. See [Controlling who can do what](08-permissions.md), or ask whoever administers your panel.
